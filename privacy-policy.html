Who we are

Carcassonne Tour is published by Touringbee Limited. You can contact us at info@touringbee.com.

What we collect

This is a static informational website. We do not ask you to create an account and we do not collect names, email addresses or payment details on this site. We use privacy-friendly, cookie-free analytics (Cloudflare Web Analytics) to understand aggregate traffic — page views, referrers and approximate country. This does not identify you personally and does not track you across other websites.

Cookies

This site does not set advertising or tracking cookies of its own. The Bókun booking window that sells the audio guide may set its own cookies when you use it. If you follow a link to a partner such as TouringBee, Tiqets, GetYourGuide, Headout or Booking.com, that third party may set its own cookies under its own privacy policy.

Purchases

When you buy the Carcassonne audio guide through the booking window on this site, your order details (name, email address, payment) are processed by Bókun and Stripe on behalf of Touringbee Limited to send you the activation email. This site never sees your payment details. We keep order records for as long as tax law requires.

Some outbound links are affiliate links. If you buy through them we may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. See our Affiliate Disclosure for details. Purchases and bookings are handled entirely by those partners, who process any personal and payment data under their own policies.

Your rights
